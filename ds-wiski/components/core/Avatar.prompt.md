Round or square reviewer avatar; use it inside AvatarBlock rather than on its own.

```jsx
<Avatar type="image" size="lg" shape="circle" />
```

- `type`: `image` | `initial` — `initial` renders `initials`.
- `size`: `sm` | `md` | `lg` (lg = 40px, the size Review Card uses).
- `shape`: `circle` | `square`.
- 12 variants in the source; the image bitmap is the kit's own avatar photo.
