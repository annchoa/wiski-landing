# Wiski Landing

The marketing landing page of Wiski Photobook. It shows what Wiski sells and sends the customer to the photobook editor.

## Language

**photobook**:
A printed album made from the customer's photos.
_Avoid_: album, book

**product line**:
A named family of photobooks that Wiski sells: Wiski Ruta or Wiski Momento.
_Avoid_: category, collection

**hero**:
The first section of the landing page. It shows the logo, the headline, one call to action and a background image.
_Avoid_: cover, portada, banner

**showcase**:
The section of the landing page that shows photos of printed photobooks in one horizontal strip.
_Avoid_: gallery

**step**:
One of the five actions the customer does to get a photobook: choose, upload, personalize, pay, wait.
_Avoid_: phase, stage

**testimonial**:
An opinion from a customer about a photobook they received, shown with a photo of that photobook.
_Avoid_: review
